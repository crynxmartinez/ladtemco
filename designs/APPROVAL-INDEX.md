## Approval notation

Review by screen ID and respond with one of:

- `Approve LAD-member-01`
- `Revise LAD-admin-11: <requested change>`
- `Reject LAD-admin-24: <reason>`

Approved PNGs will be copied into `designs/approved`. Replaced versions will be retained in `designs/superseded`.
